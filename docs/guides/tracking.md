# Tracking & Scrobbling Guide

ShinKu allows you to synchronize your reading progress with popular online tracker services so your manga list stays updated automatically as you finish chapters.

---

## 🌟 Supported Tracker Services

ShinKu supports direct synchronization with:

* **MyAnimeList (MAL)**
* **AniList**
* **Kitsu**
* **Shikimori**
* **Bangumi**
* **MangaUpdates**

---

## 🔑 How to Connect a Tracker

1. Navigate to **More → Settings → Tracking**.
2. Select your desired tracking service (e.g., *AniList* or *MyAnimeList*).
3. Tap **Log in**.
4. ShinKu will open an in-app OAuth authorization page. Log in with your tracker credentials and authorize ShinKu.
5. Once authorized, the status will show **Logged In**.

---

## 📖 Binding Manga to a Tracker

When you open a manga detail page in ShinKu:

1. Swipe over to the **Tracking** tab (or tap the Tracking icon).
2. ShinKu will perform an automated title search on your connected tracking service.
3. Tap the matching title to bind it.
4. Set your current reading status (*Reading*, *Completed*, *On Hold*, *Dropped*, *Plan to Read*) and your score rating if desired.

::: tip Automatic Scrobbling
Whenever you finish reading a chapter, ShinKu will automatically update your read count on the linked tracker in the background.
:::

---

## ⚙️ Tracking Preferences

In **More → Settings → Tracking**, you can customize:
* **Update tracker when reading:** Enable or disable real-time progress syncing.
* **Auto-track matching titles:** Automatically search and associate tracker entries when adding new manga to your library.
