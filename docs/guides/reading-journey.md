# Reading Journey & Velocity Stats

Track your reading habits, celebrate milestones, and visualize your progress with ShinKu's **Reading Journey** dashboard.

---

## 📊 Accessing Your Reading Journey

1. Open ShinKu and navigate to the **More** tab.
2. Tap the **Reading Journey** overview card at the top.

---

## 📈 What Metrics Are Tracked?

* **Reading Streaks:** Keep track of consecutive days read.
* **Reading Velocity:** See your daily, weekly, and monthly chapter consumption trends.
* **Top Genres & Demographics:** Visual distribution of what types of stories (Shonen, Seinen, Isekai, Romance) you read most.
* **Milestones & Badges:** Unlock achievements as you pass reading milestones (e.g., *100 Chapters Read*, *First Completed Series*, *Night Owl Reader*).

::: info 100% Private & Client-Side
All Reading Journey analytics are computed locally on your device from your local SQLite reading database. No statistics or reading data are ever transmitted to external servers.
:::
