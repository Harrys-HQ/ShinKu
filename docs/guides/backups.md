# Backups & Restoration Guide

ShinKu features a robust, backward-compatible backup system supporting `.tachibk` archives. You can effortlessly back up your entire reading history, custom categories, trackings, and settings—or migrate seamlessly from **Mihon**, **TachiyomiSY**, or legacy **Tachiyomi**.

---

## 💾 Creating a Backup

1. In ShinKu, navigate to **More → Settings → Data and storage**.
2. Under the **Backup and restore** section, tap **Create backup**.
3. Choose what to include:
   * **Library entries & reading history** (always included)
   * **Categories**
   * **Tracking tokens & bindings**
   * **Custom settings & reader configuration**
4. Tap **Create**. ShinKu will generate an encrypted or compressed `.tachibk` file in your selected backup directory.

---

## 🔄 Restoring a Backup

1. Navigate to **More → Settings → Data and storage**.
2. Tap **Restore backup**.
3. Select your `.tachibk` file using the system file picker.
4. Confirm the restore prompt. ShinKu will reconstruct your categories, rebind your trackers, and populate your library.

::: tip Cross-App Migration
ShinKu retains full backwards compatibility with `.tachibk` files generated by **Mihon**, **TachiyomiSY**, and **Tachiyomi**. You can restore backups directly from any of these apps into ShinKu without any manual conversion!
:::

---

## ⏰ Automated Scheduled Backups

Never worry about losing your progress:

1. Go to **More → Settings → Data and storage → Automatic backups**.
2. Set your backup schedule frequency (*Every 6 hours*, *Daily*, *Every 2 days*, or *Weekly*).
3. Set your **Max auto backups** retention limit (e.g., keep the last 5 backups) to conserve device storage.
