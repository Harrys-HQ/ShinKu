# Library & The Sanctuary Deck

The Library is the heart of ShinKu. Designed from the ground up to eliminate clutter, ShinKu combines the **Sanctuary Jump-Back Deck**, the **Floating Glass Dock**, and the **Smart Categorizer** for fluid navigation.

---

## 🏮 The Sanctuary Jump-Back Deck

At the top of your Library lives the **Sanctuary Jump-Back Deck**—a sleek, dynamic carousel that surfaces your most recently read manga titles with high-resolution artwork and quick-resume buttons.

* **Fluid Auto-Collapse:** As you scroll down through your library categories, the Jump-Back Deck smoothly collapses out of view while keeping your category tabs permanently pinned and accessible.
* **Instant Resume:** Tap the "Resume" badge on any card to immediately jump straight into the exact page you last read.

---

## 💎 Floating Glass Navigation Dock

ShinKu replaces rigid, opaque bottom navigation bars with a modern **Floating Glass Dock**:

* **Squircle Island Design:** Soft, rounded pill geometry that floats gently above content with true edge-to-edge transparency.
* **Auto-Hide on Scroll:** Keep your eyes on the art—the dock automatically tucks away when scrolling through large collections and reappears with a subtle upward flick.

---

## 🏷️ Smart Categorizer & Organization

Keep even massive 1,000+ title libraries organized without manual sorting fatigue:

1. **Category Tabs:** Organize your manga into custom tabs (*Reading*, *Backlog*, *Completed*, *On Hold*).
2. **Smart Categorizer:** Automatically assign manga to categories based on reading status, unread chapter count, or genre tags.
3. **Badges & Counters:** Display unread chapter counts, downloaded indicators, and tracker completion percentages directly on manga covers.
