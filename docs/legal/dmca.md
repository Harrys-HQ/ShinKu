# DMCA & Intellectual Property Disclaimer

ShinKu is committed to respecting intellectual property rights and adhering strictly to applicable international copyright laws, including the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

---

## 🛡️ Software Nature: Neutral Client Application

1. **Self-Contained Offline Utility:**
   ShinKu is an open-source, client-side document and media viewer application. It is designed to act purely as an extensible browsing shell and local file organizer, akin to generic web browsers (e.g., Firefox, Chromium) or media players (e.g., VLC).

2. **No Content Hosting or Distribution:**
   ShinKu does **not** host, store, index, stream, cache, or distribute any digital comic books, manga, manhwa, webtoons, light novels, or images on its servers. No media files pass through or reside on any servers affiliated with ShinKu.

3. **No Affiliation with Content Providers:**
   The developers and maintainers of ShinKu are not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with any manga publishers, scanlation groups, translation teams, or third-party web services.

4. **Zero Pre-Packaged or Curated Feeds:**
   ShinKu does not bundle, curate, recommend, or maintain any default third-party content directories, scrapers, or repositories. Users alone are responsible for configuring and connecting external data sources.

---

## ⚖️ DMCA Notice & Takedown Requests

Because ShinKu does not host, control, or serve any copyrighted media, **we cannot remove content hosted on external, third-party websites or community servers.**

If you are a copyright owner or an agent thereof and believe that content hosted on an external website infringes upon your copyright, please contact the hosting service or webmaster directly responsible for that content.

However, if you believe that any material within the official ShinKu source code repository or documentation directly infringes upon your copyright, please submit a formal notice to:

* **GitHub DMCA Procedure:** [GitHub DMCA Takedown Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy)
* **Direct Repository Inquiry:** Submit an issue under the [ShinKu Repository](https://github.com/Harrys-HQ/ShinKu/issues) marked with the `legal` label.

Please ensure your request contains:
- Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
- Identification of the specific material within this repository claimed to be infringing.
- Sufficient contact information (name, address, email address).
- A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
