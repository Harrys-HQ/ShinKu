## Confirmed findings and changes

- Crop selection at an image edge could extend beyond normalized bounds. A later resize could pass an inverted range to `coerceIn`. Drawing/resizing now uses a bounded minimum-size selection; unit regressions cover the bottom-right edge, regular coordinates and drags outside the image.
- The crop action row clipped Translate to one letter at actual 1.6× text. Actions now wrap into rows; reset/close controls have 48dp layout sizes. Existing Cancel/Details/Translate callbacks and pixel-crop conversion remain.
- The area-result title was clipped and a long language label displaced its copy control. The title wraps; card headings reserve copy-button space and copy controls use 48dp sizes. Original/translated payloads and callbacks remain.
- Chapter progress squeezed percentages into a vertical column and long language badges crowded completed counts, hiding later controls. Progress labels wrap, metadata stacks, the body scrolls, and the overlay row exposes one labelled switch action. Background/Cancel behavior is unchanged.
- The language picker lost its dropdown arrow at large text and clipped the target field on a short landscape viewport. Selected text reserves arrow space and the body scrolls; draft selection, supported-language normalization, Cancel and Confirm behavior remain.
- Native ImageView overlays ignored letterboxing, padding and original OCR dimensions. A rendering regression failed against the old mapping. Mapping now follows the image matrix after adjusting source dimensions. SSIV/Webtoon mapping paths are unchanged.
- Long dialogue in a tiny bubble painted 271 pixels outside the allowed mask/border in the regression fixture. Text is now clipped to its bubble; masks without room for the minimum padding skip typesetting. Stored dialogue remains available to the inspector/copy flow.
